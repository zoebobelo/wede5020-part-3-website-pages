About Us

Liliso Ntloko

About the Owner

My name is Liliso Ntloko, the creative eye behind the lens of Lexhaphotography. Photography is more than just a job for me — it's my way of telling stories. Growing up in Queenstown, I saw how many beautiful moments in our community go uncaptured because professional photography felt too far or too expensive. That inspired me to start Lexhaphotography.

I am a self-taught photographer based in Komani, Queenstown, Eastern Cape. I specialize in natural light photography, creative posing, and professional editing. My focus is on making sure every client, whether it's a nervous matric student or a big family, feels confident , comfortable, and beautiful during the shoot.

What makes me different is patience. I don't rush the session. I take time to understand what my client wants and I guide them until we get the perfect shot. From the first consultation on WhatsApp to the final delivery of your pictures, I ensure you get a professional and friendly experience.Lexhaphotography is not just about taking pictures — it's about preserving memories for the people of the Eastern Cape.

Our Story

What started as taking pictures for friends and family with a phone, grew through Instagram and word-of-mouth into a trusted photography service in Queenstown. In 2021, during matric, I saved up for my first professional camera and started doing free shoots to build my portfolio.By 2023, Lexhaphotography was officially registered. The name "Lexha" comes from my childhood nickname. Today, we have photographed over 150 clients — from matric balls to traditional weddings, from newborn shoots to corporate events. We currently operate mainly in Komani and surrounding areas, but we travel across the Eastern Cape for bookings.Our goal has always been the same: to make professional photography affordable, comfortable, and memorable for everyone in our community.
